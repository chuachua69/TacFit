TacFit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

TacFit ("the app") is a tactical fitness assessment and training tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We keep it minimal on purpose: TacFit is a personal training tool, not an advertising product.

Who we are

TacFit is an independent app developed by a solo developer. For any privacy question or a data-deletion request, contact: chuazhishengczs@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Google account email & basic profile — only if you choose to sign in with Google. Used solely to create your account and sync your data across devices. We receive your email address, name, and profile picture from Google; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Your training data — the numbers you enter: bodyweight, lift maxes (1RM/8RM), assessment results, logged workout sessions, custom exercises, and program start date. This is the content of the app.
  • App preferences — settings such as units and sound/haptics.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or any biometric/health-sensor data. TacFit contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How your data is stored

  • On your device — all training data is saved locally in your browser/app storage so the app works offline. If you never sign in, your data never leaves your device.
  • In the cloud (only if you sign in) — your data is synced to Supabase, our database provider, so you can access it on other devices. It is stored in a private row protected by row-level security and readable only by your account.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is processed only by the service providers that run the app:

  • Google — sign-in (OAuth). See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — authentication & database hosting.
  • Vercel — serves the web app. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can:

  • Clear local data — use "Reset to defaults" in Settings, or sign out, which clears the data stored on your device.
  • Delete your cloud account & data — email chuazhishengczs@gmail.com from your account address and we will permanently delete your synced data within 30 days.

Children

TacFit is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL.
